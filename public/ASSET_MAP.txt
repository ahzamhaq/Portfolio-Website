IMPLEMENTATION
- Do not regenerate or replace these images.
- Use the crawl frames as an animation sequence rather than a CSS cursor image.
- Use pointing_01.png when hovering links/buttons.
- Keep the cursor hotspot at the pointing fingertip.
- The crawling hand should follow the mouse with a slight delay.
- Reverse/rotate the visual toward the direction of mouse movement.
- Speed up the crawl animation when mouse velocity increases.
- Stop on settle/idle when the mouse stops.
